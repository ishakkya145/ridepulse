Inside the helmet

What you carry
on the road.

The connection
An ESP32 controller sends helmet status and events to your phone over Bluetooth.
The sound
Two speakers sit inside the helmet. A built-in microphone picks up your voice for calls.
The electronics
The circuit board sits at the back of the helmet alongside the amplifier that drives the speakers.

Emergency alerts

Twenty seconds
to say “I’m okay.”

If the helmet reports a possible accident or SOS, Helmet Alert starts a countdown. If you’re safe, you can cancel it on your phone or helmet.

When the countdown ends, the app attempts to notify your verified emergency contacts with the event time and your phone’s available location.

Detection and delivery depend on the helmet connection, phone permissions and available service. The app does not dispatch emergency responders.

First ride

A few minutes before you leave.

Do the first setup while parked.

  1. Install the app

    Download the Android APK, then open it from Downloads. If Android asks, allow installation for the browser or file app you used.

  2. Choose your people

    Set up your rider profile. Add and verify your emergency contacts, then select your alert destinations.

  3. Pair with RidePulse

    Power on your helmet. In your phone’s Bluetooth settings, select RidePulse.

  4. Connect the helmet

    Open Helmet Alert and tap Connect helmet. Grant the permissions for the features you plan to use.

  5. Check your position

    In Alerts, check the map, location accuracy and last update. Confirm your contacts are ready and keep Bluetooth and location on during the ride.

Rider support

Field notes.

How do I update my app?

Come back to this page, download the latest APK and install it over your existing compatible version. Direct downloads require you to confirm each update. This Android prototype was published on 26 November 2026. View release details and file checksum.

Previous version: Download Helmet Alert 1.0 · 1.0 checksum.

How do I get the map working?

In Alerts, start a location preview and select CARTO. Tap Get free CARTO key, follow the provider’s instructions, then paste your key under Enter CARTO key. The map follows your phone’s location. CARTO key setup.

Which features need the companion app?

Use Helmet Alert for the helmet’s data connection, ride recording, location sharing, countdowns and contact alerts. Pairing for audio alone won’t activate sensor alerts.

Can I try it without my helmet?

Preview your phone’s location or use headset test mode to check Bluetooth audio. You can also run the 20-second simulation from Home. Let your contacts know first because it can send test messages.

How does the simulated battery level work?

Version 2.0 shows a simulated 74% when the helmet connects. It drops one percentage point every five connected minutes and resets on reconnection.

Can I install it on my phone?

Helmet Alert runs on Android 7.0 and later. An iPhone version is unavailable. If Android blocks the APK, check the installation permission for the app opening the file and keep Play Protect enabled.

What does this website collect?

This page uses no tracking cookies, analytics or sign-in forms. The host may keep standard access logs. The Android app uses its own account services and permissions.
